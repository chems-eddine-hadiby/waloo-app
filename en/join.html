Become a partner

Join the Waloo network.

Whether you're a shop owner or want to become a cash-in agent, signing up is simple and fast.

🏪 Become a Store

Sell your surplus and unsold items to customers near you. Reduce waste while generating extra revenue.

01

Download the app

Install Waloo from the Play Store or App Store and create your account.

02

Choose the "Store" role

During sign-up, select the Store role to access merchant features.

03

Complete KYC

Provide your trade register and a valid ID. Verification typically takes 24 to 48 hours.

04

Set up your profile

Add your store name, exact address, pin your location on the map, set opening hours, and upload your logo.

05

Start listing items

Begin listing your surplus: add photos, discounted price, available quantity, and pickup window.

✓ Store benefits

Increased local visibility, reduced waste, revenue from unsold goods, analytics dashboard, real-time booking notifications.

👤 Become an Agent

Become a Waloo cash-in point in your neighborhood. Let users top up their balance with cash.

01

Download the app

Install Waloo and create your account with your phone number.

02

Choose the "Agent" role

Select the Agent role during sign-up to access cash-in tools.

03

Complete KYC

Provide a valid ID document. Your application will be reviewed within 24 to 48 hours.

04

Set up your checkpoint

Pin your location on the map so nearby users can find you for cash-in operations.

05

Start operations

Receive top-up requests, collect cash, and credit users' balances through the app.

✓ Agent benefits

Commission on every transaction, flexible hours, no commercial space required, transaction dashboard.
